Principal engineering

Technical ambition, grounded in people.

From uncertainty to useful software

I create the conditions for teams to move.

I drive projects from the first uncertain conversation through to reliable delivery. That means shaping technical direction, making trade-offs visible, and helping people build with confidence.

I like ambitious technology paired with practical guardrails: security treated as design material, observability built in early, and decisions explained clearly enough to earn trust.

AI adoption, with judgment

The model is only one piece of the system.

I help teams understand where LLMs are genuinely useful, where they are not, and how to fit them into real development workflows without losing sight of security, privacy, or the people doing the work.

The field changes daily. I find that energising. Every project is a chance to learn, test an assumption, and build something more capable than what came before.
